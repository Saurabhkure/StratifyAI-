Prototype Summary – Signal Extraction from Market & News Data
This prototype builds an AI-driven system that merges real-time market data and financial news to extract trading signals and validate strategies. Using n8n, stock prices from Alpha Vantage and news articles are ingested into Snowflake. Cortex performs NLP and sentiment analysis, mapping articles to tickers. Snowpark generates features like returns, volatility, and sentiment interactions. A backtesting engine simulates trading actions, saving performance metrics. Each signal is explainable, linked to its originating event or news. The fully automated workflow is production-ready, scalable, and seamlessly operates within the Snowflake ecosystem.
